\begin{center}
{\LARGE \textbf{Radix-4 Booth, 2 PPs per slice}}\\[4pt]
{\large an 8-bit signed $B$ split into two nibbles, recoded across the nibbles}
\end{center}

\vspace{3em}

\noindent\textbf{Step 1 --- split $B$ into two nibbles}

\noindent $H$ is the value of the high nibble read as signed, $L$ the value of the low nibble
read as unsigned, $b_3$ the top bit of $L$:
\[
B \;=\; 16\,H + L
\qquad\qquad
H \in [-8, 7]
\qquad
L \in [0, 15]
\]

\noindent Today: $H$ is signed and takes 2 Booth digits, $L$ is unsigned and takes 3.

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent\textbf{Step 2 --- read the low nibble as signed}

\noindent Booth reads every nibble as signed, so the low nibble comes out with its top bit
counting $-8$ instead of $+8$, that is $16\,b_3$ too small:
\[
L' \;=\; L - 16\,b_3
\]

\noindent Two digits, no third one. On its own $L'$ is wrong by $-16\,b_3$.

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent\textbf{Step 3 --- give the missing $16\,b_3$ to the high nibble}

\noindent Booth's first window of the high nibble has a ``bit below''. Feed it $b_3$ instead of
$0$: that adds $+1$ to the high nibble, i.e. $+16$ to $B$, exactly when $b_3 = 1$.
\[
H' \;=\; H + b_3
\qquad\qquad
16\,H' + L' \;=\; 16\,H + 16\,b_3 + L - 16\,b_3 \;=\; B
\]

\noindent $H'$ can be $8$, one more than a nibble holds, but two Booth digits reach $10$.
Four digits for $B$, two per nibble; the bit below is $0$ for the lowest nibble or a whole
4-bit operand, and the top bit of the nibble below otherwise.

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent\textbf{Step 4 --- the dot product}

\noindent The low nibbles go to one DP8, the high nibbles to another; the tree adds the high
DP8 shifted by 16:
\[
\sum_{i} A_i \, B_i
  \;=\; 16 \underbrace{\sum_{i} A_i \, H'_i}_{\textstyle \text{high DP8}}
  \;+\; \underbrace{\sum_{i} A_i \, L'_i}_{\textstyle \text{low DP8}}
\]

\noindent Each DP8 alone is off by the same term $E = \sum_{i} b_{3,i} \, A_i$, with opposite signs
and weights:
\[
\text{low DP8} \;=\; \sum_{i} A_i \, L_i \;-\; 16\,E
\qquad\qquad
\text{high DP8} \;=\; \sum_{i} A_i \, H_i \;+\; E
\]

\noindent The $\times 16$ of the tree turns $+E$ into $+16\,E$ and cancels the $-16\,E$.

\newpage

\noindent\textbf{Step 5 --- adding several DP8s first}

\noindent Adding all low DP8s together, all high DP8s together, and then the two sums:
\[
\Bigl[\, \text{low}_1 + \text{low}_2 \,\Bigr] + 16 \Bigl[\, \text{high}_1 + \text{high}_2 \,\Bigr]
\;=\; \sum_{i} A_i \bigl( L'_i + 16\,H'_i \bigr)
\;=\; \sum_{i} A_i \, B_i
\]

\noindent The error is $-16\,b_{3,i} A_i$ on the low side and $+16\,b_{3,i} A_i$ on the high side for
every element $i$, so it cancels whatever the grouping, as long as both nibbles of each
element end up in the same sum with the high one weighted 16.
